Project Overview

Our capstone team designed a system to move a wind-tunnel model’s drag reduction system between testing positions without disturbing its aerodynamic surfaces.

We modeled the airfoil surface in Python and MATLAB to explore and optimize the system’s design parameters. With testing constraints limiting direct validation, we evaluated the design under adapted conditions and analyzed its performance.

Focus
Mechanical design, automation, and mathematical modeling
Tools
Python · NumPy · SciPy · scikit-learn · MATLAB
